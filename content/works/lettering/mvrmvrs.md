Logo and symbol for the small game publisher MVRMVRS, which curates adventure and RPG indie video games with lo-fi or occult themes.  

The logo was sketched in pencil, then in ink and cleaned up and textured digitally.

The brief was:  
> 'Folk horror meets black metal in the forest for a D&D game'

Logo and symbol for the band Fleshbrand from Bellingham, USA.  
Their music is a blend of hardcore and death metal with shades of industrial.  

The logo was sketched in pencil, then in ink and cleaned up and textured digitally.

The brief was:  
> 'Industrial, gritty, and kind of imparts that old school Survival Horror vibe with the look, while also being “hard af” as the kids say and pointy.'
